# Phase 3B — Contextual Narrative Verification

## Goal
Prevent locally attractive clips from being accepted when their surrounding narrative promise, dependency, or payoff is lost.

## Included
- bounded before/after transcript windows
- post-candidate resolution search
- promise→payoff relation proxy
- context integrity and dependency scoring
- premature-cut and unresolved-question risks
- contextual recommendation and confidence
- AnalysisWorld enrichment and provenance
- intent-aware selection consumes contextual evidence
- regression tests

## Design rule
Contextual verification is additive evidence. Existing narrative, intent, multimodal ranking, and AI rejudge remain intact. No superiority claim is made without benchmark validation.

## Next
Adaptive context expansion, semantic/local embedding verification, cross-speaker reconstruction, contradiction/reference resolution, and persistent narrative graph support.
