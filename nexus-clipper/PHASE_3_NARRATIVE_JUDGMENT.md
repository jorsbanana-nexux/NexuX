# Phase 3 — Narrative Understanding & Editorial Judgment

Phase 3 upgrades NexuX from candidate scoring toward explicit narrative understanding.

## Slice 1
- Narrative assessment contract
- Story beats: setup, promise, tension, revelation, consequence/payoff
- Context completeness
- Standalone quality
- Continuity risk
- Premature-cut risk
- Unresolved-question risk
- Explainable editorial recommendation: KEEP / REFINE / REJECT
- Confidence and evidence

## Rule
Narrative judgment supplements existing multimodal ranking. It does not replace the deterministic ranker or AI rejudge until benchmark evidence shows that migration is safe.

## Next
Persist narrative assessment in AnalysisWorld, use it in final candidate selection, then add multi-window context retrieval and true promise→payoff verification.
