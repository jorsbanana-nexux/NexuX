# NexuX No-B-roll Policy

NexuX intentionally does not fetch, generate, synthesize, or automatically insert B-roll.

The product focuses on transforming the source video itself:

- semantic clip selection
- silence/filler/repetition cleanup
- subject-aware reframing
- dynamic virtual camera
- deterministic captions
- headline/emoji metadata
- audio preservation and normalization through the canonical FFmpeg pipeline
- multi-platform aspect-ratio export

The existing B-roll blocker remains a safety guard, not a feature source. This policy is a hard product constraint for the V5 canonical pipeline.
